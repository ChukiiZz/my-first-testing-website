The Art Of Drawing

Drawing is a visual art in which an artist or anyone uses tools like pencils, pens, charcoal, ink or any kinds to create images on a surface.

Drawings can be realistic or imaginative. This helps develop creativity, observation skills, and hand-eye coordination.


Technique How It works Tools Used
Sketching Creates a rough outline of the subject. Pencil, paper
Shading Adds light and dark tones to create depth. Pencil, charcoal, blending stump
Blending Smooths shading for a softer, realistic look. Blending stump, tissue, cotton swab


There's a lot of drawings you do, and one the best way to draw is having a reference. There are some apps or sites you could visit so you could have some reference to draw like Pinterest, Tumblr or other.

Although, I recommend using Pinterest. It is one of the best to get art references.
