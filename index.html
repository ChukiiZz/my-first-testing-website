The World Of Video Games

Video game is a visual electronic game where a player control images on a video screen using an input device like a controller, keyboard, or touchscreen.

Video games are a form of digital entertainment that people of all ages enjoy.

They can be played on computers, gaming consoles, smartphones, and tablets. Over the years, video games have become more advanced, offering realistic graphics, exciting stories, and interactive gameplay. Today, millions of people around the world play video games for fun, learning, and social interaction.
